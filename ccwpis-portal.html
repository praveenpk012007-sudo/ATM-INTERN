About the Framework

Understanding the Cybercrime Cash Withdrawal Prediction & Intervention System (CCWPIS)

From Reactive Response to Proactive Interception

The traditional approach to combating cybercrime-linked cash withdrawals has been inherently reactive — action is initiated only after a complaint is filed and funds have often already been siphoned. The CCWPIS framework fundamentally shifts this paradigm.

By leveraging AI/ML models trained on historical complaint data from the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP), the system predicts likely cash withdrawal hotspots — specific ATMs, bank branches, and geographic clusters — before fraudulent withdrawals occur.

I4C's Coordination Role

Operating under the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, CCWPIS serves as the predictive intelligence backbone integrated with the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS).

  • Real-time data ingestion from NCRP complaint pipeline
  • Integration with CFCFRMS for end-to-end fund tracing
  • Coordination with state/UT cyber cells and LEAs
  • Automated alerts to banks & financial institutions for proactive fund blocking
  • GIS-enabled risk heatmaps with drill-down analytics
